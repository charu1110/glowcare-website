Our story

Born in a Bengaluru kitchen, tested on real weather.

GlowCare started when our founder, a chemical engineer with stubbornly reactive skin, got tired of choosing between formulas that were gentle and formulas that worked. Six years later we still make everything in small batches — because a serum should smell like nothing and do everything.

We publish the full ingredient list and percentage of every active on the label, price honestly, and refill instead of repackage. No filters, no fine print.
